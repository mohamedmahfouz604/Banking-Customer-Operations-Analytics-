📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Banking Analytics Dashboard built in Power BI to provide decision-makers with a comprehensive view of the bank's operations.

Instead of focusing only on visualizations, the project started by understanding the banking domain, identifying meaningful KPIs, and translating business questions into interactive dashboards.

The report covers six major business areas:

Executive Overview
Transactions
Customer Analytics
Customer Support
Loans

🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer questions such as:

How is the bank performing overall?
Which account types hold the highest balances?
How are customers distributed across different states?
What transaction patterns exist throughout the year?
How effective is customer support?
Which loan types generate the highest lending volume?
How healthy is the bank's card portfolio?

💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

Customer Support
Improve the ticket resolution process.
Investigate the root causes of unresolved cases.
Prioritize Transaction Dispute handling since it generates the highest support demand.
Customer Growth
Launch targeted campaigns in states with lower customer counts.
Encourage customers with one account to open additional products.
Transactions
Analyze the highest transaction accounts for potential VIP or Premium Banking opportunities.
Investigate seasonal changes to optimize staffing and operational planning.
Loans
Expand successful loan products such as Personal and Home loans.
Review Business Loan pricing and customer adoption.
Promote medium-term loan products while monitoring portfolio risk.
Cards
Launch automated renewal campaigns for cards expiring within the next 12 months.
Increase card penetration among customers without cards.
Cross-sell credit cards to existing account holders.

📈 Project Outcome

This project demonstrates how raw banking data can be transformed into meaningful business insights that support decision-making across multiple departments, including operations, customer service, lending, and retail banking.

Rather than creating dashboards for visualization alone, the focus was on answering real business questions and presenting actionable insights through a clean, interactive Power BI solution.
